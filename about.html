About Our System

Our website is designed to help users understand different diseases found in animals. It's a simple, easy-to-use platform for both pet owners and livestock caretakers to access helpful disease information.

Our Mission

Spreading Awareness About Animal Health

We aim to share basic knowledge about common animal diseases so people can take better care of their animals and understand symptoms early.

What Our Website Offers

Key Features

Informative Disease Details

Read about common diseases in animals like cows, goats, sheep, dogs, cats, and rabbits.

Easy Language

All disease information is explained in simple words so it's easy for everyone to understand.

Helpful for All

Whether you're a student, a pet owner, or a farmer, this website can help you learn and stay informed.
