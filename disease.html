Animal Diseases Encyclopedia

Browse our comprehensive list of common animal diseases and their treatments

Bovine Respiratory Disease (BRD) High

Common in: Cattle

Symptoms: Fever, coughing, nasal discharge, breathing difficulty, loss of appetite

Treatment: Antibiotics (like oxytetracycline), NSAIDs for fever, improved ventilation

Prevention: Vaccination, stress reduction, proper nutrition

Johne's Disease Moderate

Common in: Cattle, Goats, Sheep

Symptoms: Chronic diarrhea, weight loss despite good appetite, weakness

Treatment: No effective treatment, management focuses on preventing spread

Prevention: Culling infected animals, calf hygiene, testing program

Peacock Pox Moderate

Common in: Peacocks, Peahens

Symptoms: Skin lesions on unfeathered areas, difficulty eating

Treatment: Supportive care, topical treatments for lesions

Prevention: Vaccination, mosquito control

Rabies High

Common in: Dogs, Cats

Symptoms: Aggression, foaming at the mouth, paralysis

Treatment: Post-exposure vaccination

Prevention: Vaccination, avoiding contact with wild animals

Foot and Mouth Disease High

Common in: Cattle, Sheep, Goats

Symptoms: Blisters in the mouth, fever, limping

Treatment: No specific treatment, supportive care

Prevention: Vaccination, quarantine measures

Avian Influenza High

Common in: Poultry (Chickens, Ducks)

Symptoms: Sudden death, respiratory issues, loss of appetite

Treatment: Antiviral drugs (if caught early), supportive care

Prevention: Vaccination, biosecurity practices

Canine Parvovirus High

Common in: Dogs

Symptoms: Vomiting, diarrhea, lethargy

Treatment: Supportive care, fluids, anti-nausea medication

Prevention: Vaccination

Leptospirosis High

Common in: Dogs, Rodents

Symptoms: Fever, vomiting, lethargy, kidney damage

Treatment: Antibiotics (like doxycycline), supportive care

Prevention: Vaccination, rodent control

Avian Influenza High

Common in: Poultry

Symptoms: Sudden death, respiratory distress, drop in egg production

Treatment: Antiviral medications (in early stages)

Prevention: Quarantine, vaccination, biosecurity measures

Mastitis High

Common in: Dairy Cows

Symptoms: Swollen udders, reduced milk production, abnormal milk

Treatment: Antibiotics, proper milking hygiene

Prevention: Proper milking technique, regular cleaning, culling infected cows

Foot and Mouth Disease High

Common in: Cattle, Sheep, Goats

Symptoms: Fever, sores on mouth and hooves, limping

Treatment: No cure, supportive care, isolation

Prevention: Vaccination, biosecurity measures

Rabies High

Common in: Dogs, Cats, Wild Animals

Symptoms: Aggression, foaming at the mouth, paralysis

Treatment: Post-exposure prophylaxis (vaccination), no treatment after symptoms appear

Prevention: Vaccination, avoiding contact with wild animals

Canine Parvovirus High

Common in: Dogs

Symptoms: Vomiting, diarrhea (often bloody), dehydration

Treatment: Supportive care, fluids, antivirals

Prevention: Vaccination, hygiene

Feline Leukemia High

Common in: Cats

Symptoms: Weight loss, fever, anemia, lethargy

Treatment: No cure, supportive care

Prevention: Vaccination, testing

Bovine Tuberculosis High

Common in: Cattle

Symptoms: Coughing, weight loss, reduced milk production

Treatment: Antibiotics (not always effective)

Prevention: Testing, culling infected animals

Equine Influenza Moderate

Common in: Horses

Symptoms: Coughing, nasal discharge, fever

Treatment: Rest, anti-inflammatory medications

Prevention: Vaccination

Heartworm High

Common in: Dogs

Symptoms: Coughing, lethargy, difficulty breathing

Treatment: Antiparasitic drugs

Prevention: Monthly preventatives

Canine Distemper High

Common in: Dogs

Symptoms: Fever, coughing, vomiting, seizures

Treatment: Supportive care, fluids

Prevention: Vaccination

Toxoplasmosis Moderate

Common in: Cats

Symptoms: Fever, lethargy, muscle pain

Treatment: Antiprotozoal medications

Prevention: Avoid contact with infected litter, cook meat thoroughly

Leptospirosis High

Common in: Dogs, Livestock

Symptoms: Fever, vomiting, abdominal pain, jaundice

Treatment: Antibiotics

Prevention: Vaccination, avoiding exposure to contaminated water

Psittacosis Moderate

Common in: Parrots, Birds

Symptoms: Respiratory issues, lethargy, watery eyes

Treatment: Antibiotics

Prevention: Good hygiene, avoiding bird droppings

Rabies High

Common in: All Animals

Symptoms: Fever, headache, excessive salivation, muscle spasms, paralysis

Treatment: Immediate vaccination, supportive care

Prevention: Vaccinate animals, avoid animal bites

Leptospirosis Moderate

Common in: Livestock, Dogs

Symptoms: Fever, vomiting, abdominal pain, jaundice, kidney damage

Treatment: Antibiotics, supportive care

Prevention: Avoid contact with contaminated water, vaccination

Bovine Tuberculosis High

Common in: Cattle

Symptoms: Coughing, weight loss, fever, fatigue

Treatment: Antibiotics, but difficult to treat in cattle

Prevention: Test and remove infected animals, pasteurization of milk

Mastitis Moderate

Common in: Cattle

Symptoms: Swollen udders, reduced milk production, heat in udders

Treatment: Antibiotics, milking management

Prevention: Proper milking hygiene, clean equipment

Avian Influenza High

Common in: Poultry

Symptoms: Fever, cough, diarrhea, sudden death

Treatment: Antiviral drugs in early stages

Prevention: Vaccination, biosecurity measures

Equine Infectious Anemia High

Common in: Horses

Symptoms: Fever, anemia, swelling in legs, weakness

Treatment: No cure, euthanasia for severe cases

Prevention: Testing, vector control

Brucellosis Moderate

Common in: Cattle, Sheep, Goats

Symptoms: Fever, joint pain, weight loss, abortion in pregnant animals

Treatment: Antibiotics, but chronic in some cases

Prevention: Vaccination, testing and culling infected animals

Cysticercosis Moderate

Common in: Pigs

Symptoms: Abdominal pain, nausea, seizures (in humans)

Treatment: Surgery or anti-parasitic drugs

Prevention: Prevent ingestion of contaminated food or water

Anthrax High

Common in: Cattle, Sheep

Symptoms: Sudden death, fever, swelling, respiratory distress

Treatment: Antibiotics (early stage), vaccination

Prevention: Vaccination, quarantine of infected animals

Toxoplasmosis Moderate

Common in: Cats, Humans

Symptoms: Fever, muscle pain, fatigue (in humans)

Treatment: Antiparasitic medications

Prevention: Proper hygiene, avoid contact with infected cat feces
