Animal Health Awareness

Basic knowledge for pet lovers and animal caretakers

Understanding disease symptoms early can help save animals and improve their health. Our project gives easy and reliable info about many animal diseases, what signs to look for, and basic treatment options.

Please note: This is an educational project. For serious issues, always visit a real veterinarian for proper checkup and treatment.
